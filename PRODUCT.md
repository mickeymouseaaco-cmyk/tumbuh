# Tumbuh

A companion for first-time parents in Malaysia to follow their baby's teeth (0–36 months).

- **Who:** mums, dads and caregivers of a first child; many read Bahasa Melayu first. Often used at night,
  one-handed, tired. They don't want to read much.
- **What it does:** mark which baby teeth are in and see what's due; a daily mouth-care routine; picture
  lessons (cleaning, fluoride, food, teething, dentist); photos of common problems with "what to do";
  fluoride-varnish reminders every 6 months.
- **Voice:** warm, plain, never scary. Layman's Bahasa Melayu and plain English. General information, not
  a diagnosis.
- **Personality:** friendly, playful, trustworthy, premium, modern, warm, interactive, educational.
- **Platform:** Expo / React Native, shipped as a web app (GitHub → Vercel), installed to the home screen.
  Data stays on the phone; there is no account.
